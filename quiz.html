How Matching Works

The short version

Each question feeds into one of six interest & personality types — Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, and Conventional (RIASEC), a framework widely used in real career counseling. We build a profile from your answers, compare it to the same six-trait profile for every course in our catalog, and rank the closest matches — each with a plain-language reason, not just a label.
